# Véhicules

**Dans le jeu** : Voitures, utilitaire, taxi, police, bus, camion, ambulance. Deux images : vus de face, vus de dos (l'autre sens s'obtient en retournant l'image).

Carrosserie de la voiture, de l'utilitaire, du bus et du camion en **vert** : couleur repère recolorée par le programme. Taxi, police et ambulance gardent leurs vraies couleurs.

**Pièce jointe** (à chaque génération) : `ref-diorama.jpg` (référence de style et d'angle pour la ville)

## 1. vehicules-avant  — format **16:9**

```
Same art style, outlines, colour palette and soft painted shading as the attached reference image: a lush, cozy French small town, hand-painted anime mobile-game look, warm daylight from the upper left. Isometric view from a high three-quarter angle, exactly the same camera angle as the reference. Separate objects side by side, same scale, generous empty space between them so they never touch or overlap. Seven vehicles, all driving towards the lower left (front and left side visible): a small rounded 1950s French car, a delivery van, a taxi (black with a small white roof light without letters), a police car (white and blue), a city bus, a small truck, an ambulance (white with a red cross). IMPORTANT for recolouring: the painted body of the car, the van, the bus and the truck is bright saturated green (#22CC22); windows light blue glass, tyres black, chrome grey. Plain white background, no ground under the objects, no cast shadow, no text, no letters or numbers anywhere (leave every sign board blank).
```
