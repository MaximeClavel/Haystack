# Commerces

**Dans le jeu** : Boutiques sans nom (style `shop`), plus café, bar et petit hôtel ordinaires.

Murs en **magenta** et toits en **bleu vif** : ce sont des couleurs repères, le programme les remplace par les couleurs de chaque bâtiment du jeu (comme le vert/bleu des habitants). Stores rayés jaunes = couleur du store recolorée aussi.

**Pièce jointe** (à chaque génération) : `ref-batiment.jpg` (référence de style et d'angle pour un bâtiment seul)

## 1. boutiques  — format **16:9**

```
Three different two-storey buildings with a shop on the ground floor: big display window, striped awning, blank sign board above the window, flat above. Same art style, outlines, colour palette and soft painted shading as the attached reference image: a lush, cozy French small town, hand-painted anime mobile-game look, warm daylight from the upper left. Isometric view from a high three-quarter angle, exactly the same camera angle as the reference. Separate objects side by side, same scale, generous empty space between them so they never touch or overlap. The front facade with the entrance faces the lower left and the side wall faces the lower right, exactly like the reference building. IMPORTANT for recolouring: all plaster walls are painted bright saturated magenta (#FF00FF) and all roof tiles are bright saturated blue (#1E90FF); doors and shutters stay brown wood, stone corners grey, window-box flowers only red and white, ivy and moss green, awnings striped bright yellow and white. Plain white background, no ground under the objects, no cast shadow, no text, no letters or numbers anywhere (leave every sign board blank).
```

Résultat à enregistrer ici sous le nom `boutiques.png`.
