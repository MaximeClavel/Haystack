## Prompt — format **1:1**, 1600 × 1600

```
Image 1 is a crop from a larger hand-drawn isometric map of a small French town. Redraw image 1 as a sharp, high-resolution, richly detailed illustration.

KEEP EXACTLY: the same art style, outlines and colours, and the same composition and framing. Every building, roof, chimney, street, hedge and tree, every boat, the river, crate stays at exactly the same place, with the same size, the same shape and the same viewing angle. Keep the colour of every roof, wall and awning exactly as in image 1. Everything that touches an edge of image 1 touches the same edge at the same place in your image. Do not zoom in or out, do not shift, crop, rotate or extend the view, do not add or remove any building.

ADD DETAILS that were too small to see: window frames, doors, roof tiles, ivy leaves, cobblestones, flower boxes, lamp posts, benches.

ADD PEOPLE in the same hand-drawn style: about 25 small townspeople, each about as tall as a ground-floor door: onlookers on the quay, bargemen on the boats. They wear ordinary clothes and must look clearly different from the story characters: the two burglars are identical twins, the ONLY difference is the backpack; nobody else wears a beanie, sunglasses or a top hat.

STORY (draw every element exactly as described, clearly visible, never hidden behind a tree, a building or another person):
- The building with the purple awnings is a jewellery shop: put the sign "BIJOUTERIE CARAT" above the awnings. One of its display windows is smashed, with broken glass on the pavement.
- On the pavement right in front of the shop, an open manhole with its round cover pushed aside; his identical twin shown on image 2 (orange knitted beanie, sunglasses, grey hoodie, black trousers, NO backpack) is climbing out of it, only his upper body out, both hands on the edge.
- The burglar with a backpack shown on image 3 (orange knitted beanie, sunglasses, grey hoodie, black trousers, BACKPACK) tiptoes away from the manhole towards the right, bent forward, holding a sparkling diamond in one hand.
- The jeweller shown on image 4 (black top hat, round glasses, purple suit, walking cane) stands in front of the smashed window, talking on a telephone held to his ear, with a blue sweat drop above his head.
- Next to him, the policeman shown on image 5 (navy kepi, big ginger moustache, blue uniform jacket, beige Bermuda shorts, red-and-yellow striped scarf), reading his little notebook.

The only written words in the image are: "BIJOUTERIE CARAT" (spelled exactly); every other sign stays blank. No frame, no caption.
```
