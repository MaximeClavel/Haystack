## Prompt — format **1:1**, 1600 × 1600

```
Image 1 is a crop from a larger hand-drawn isometric map of a small French town. Redraw image 1 as a sharp, high-resolution, richly detailed illustration.

KEEP EXACTLY: the same art style, outlines and colours, and the same composition and framing. Every building, roof, chimney, street, hedge and tree, every the pond, the jetty, the boat, water lily stays at exactly the same place, with the same size, the same shape and the same viewing angle. Keep the colour of every roof, wall and awning exactly as in image 1. Everything that touches an edge of image 1 touches the same edge at the same place in your image. Do not zoom in or out, do not shift, crop, rotate or extend the view, do not add or remove any building.

ADD DETAILS that were too small to see: window frames, doors, roof tiles, ivy leaves, cobblestones, flower boxes, lamp posts, benches.

ADD PEOPLE in the same hand-drawn style: about 20 small townspeople, each about as tall as a ground-floor door: anglers, a couple on the grass, joggers on the path. They wear ordinary clothes and must look clearly different from the story characters: exactly ONE golden duck, every other duck is an ordinary mallard; nobody else wears a fedora, a green coat or a straw hat.

STORY (draw every element exactly as described, clearly visible, never hidden behind a tree, a building or another person):
- Write "LE BARON" on the side of the brown rowing boat. Standing in this boat, the bearded man shown on image 2 (brown fedora, full grey beard, long green checked coat, yellow trousers), both hands raised, with a blue sweat drop above his head, and four angry ordinary mallard ducks swimming around the boat.
- On the shore at the foot of the wooden jetty, the old baroness shown on image 3 (straw hat, white bun, round glasses, purple dress with white polka dots, handbag), both hands raised in despair, with a blue sweat drop above her head.
- The SAME bearded man is shown a second time (earlier in the story): crouching at the water's edge on the grassy shore between the jetty and the boat, holding out a baguette to a duck with entirely shiny golden feathers that is coming out of the water towards him.
- A little further right on the shore, the child shown on image 4 (spiky blond hair, round glasses, blue dungarees, orange shorts, red balloon), pointing at the crouching man, with two ordinary mallard ducks at his feet.
- The white marquee of the cream art deco cinema reads "LE REX".

The only written words in the image are: "LE BARON", "LE REX" (spelled exactly); every other sign stays blank. No frame, no caption.
```
