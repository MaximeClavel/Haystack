Keep the colour of every roof, wall and awning exactly as in image 1. Everything that touches an edge of image 1 touches the same edge at the same place in your image. ADD DETAILS that were too small to see: window frames, doors, roof tiles, ivy leaves, cobblestones, flower boxes, lamp posts, benches, bread, fruit and vegetables on the market stalls.

```
ADD PEOPLE in the same hand-drawn style: about 45 small townspeople, each about as tall as a ground-floor door: shoppers with baskets and stallholders in aprons at the market stalls. They wear ordinary clothes and must look clearly different from the story characters: nobody else wears a beanie, a mask, a striped sweater, a chef's toque or a police uniform.

STORY (draw every element exactly as described, clearly visible, never hidden behind a tree, a building or another person):

- Right next to her, the policeman shown on image 3 (navy kepi, big ginger moustache, blue uniform jacket, beige Bermuda shorts, red-and-yellow striped scarf), holding a baguette in one hand and looking at it lovingly, with a small red heart bubble above his head.

```
