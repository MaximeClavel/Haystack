```

KEEP EXACTLY: the same art style, outlines and colours, and the same composition and framing. Keep the colour of every roof, wall and awning exactly as in image 1. Everything that touches an edge of image 1 touches the same edge at the same place in your image. Do not zoom in or out, do not shift, crop, rotate or extend the view, do not add or remove any building.

ADD DETAILS that were too small to see: window frames, doors, roof tiles, ivy leaves, cobblestones, flower boxes, lamp posts, benches.

ADD PEOPLE in the same hand-drawn style: about 40 small townspeople, each about as tall as a ground-floor door: spectators, some holding ordinary ducks in their arms or in baskets, a small brass band in the bandstand, strollers. They wear ordinary clothes and must look clearly different from the story characters: exactly ONE golden duck; every other duck is an ordinary brown and green mallard.

STORY (draw every element exactly as described, clearly visible, never hidden behind a tree, a building or another person):
- On the lawn between the two big trees and the bandstand, the duck beauty contest: a wooden signpost reading "CONCOURS DE CANES" and a long table with velvet cushions and rosettes.
- A duck with entirely shiny golden feathers sits proudly on a cushion on the table, and next to her one ordinary mallard duck on another cushion.
- The contest president stands by the table, pointing at the golden duck, with a "!" bubble above his head: a slim elderly man with dark brown skin, short white hair and glasses, a black bowler hat and a navy striped suit, a newspaper under his arm.
- On the other side of the table, a jealous contestant with an angry red comic symbol above her head: a woman with curly grey hair, a beret, a red sweater, a brown skirt and a walking cane.

The only written words in the image are: "CONCOURS DE CANES", "LE REX" (spelled exactly); every other sign stays blank. No frame, no caption.
```
