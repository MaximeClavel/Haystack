## Prompt — format **1:1**, 1600 × 1600

```
Image 1 is a crop from a larger hand-drawn isometric map of a small French town. Redraw image 1 as a sharp, high-resolution, richly detailed illustration.

KEEP EXACTLY: the same art style, outlines and colours, and the same composition and framing. Every building, roof, chimney, street, hedge and tree, every boat, the river, the windmill stays at exactly the same place, with the same size, the same shape and the same viewing angle. Keep the colour of every roof, wall and awning exactly as in image 1. Everything that touches an edge of image 1 touches the same edge at the same place in your image. Do not zoom in or out, do not shift, crop, rotate or extend the view, do not add or remove any building.

ADD DETAILS that were too small to see: window frames, doors, roof tiles, ivy leaves, cobblestones, flower boxes, lamp posts, benches.

ADD PEOPLE in the same hand-drawn style: about 20 small townspeople, each about as tall as a ground-floor door: bargemen on the boats, anglers on the quay, a washerwoman on the lower bank. They wear ordinary clothes and must look clearly different from the story characters: nobody else wears stripes, a beanie, a beret or a pink dress.

STORY (draw every element exactly as described, clearly visible, never hidden behind a tree, a building or another person):
- On the stone quay at the top, near a mooring bollard, the sailor shown on image 2 (orange knitted beanie, spiky blond hair, blue-striped white top, grey trousers) stands holding a coil of rope in his hands.
- The small boat with a white cabin moored at the lower bank, next to the weeping willows, is painted red and has the name "LA COCOTTE" on its side. the young woman shown on image 3 (red beret, sunglasses, curly ginger-red hair, pink dress with white polka dots) stands at its helm, waving with one hand.
- The escaped convict shown on image 4 (spiky blond hair, no hat, white pyjama shirt with blue stripes, grey trousers) is climbing out of that red boat onto the lower bank, both hands on the edge of the bank.
- The sign above the door of the garage with the stacks of tyres reads "GARAGE PISTONS".

The only written words in the image are: "LA COCOTTE", "GARAGE PISTONS" (spelled exactly); every other sign stays blank. No frame, no caption.
```
