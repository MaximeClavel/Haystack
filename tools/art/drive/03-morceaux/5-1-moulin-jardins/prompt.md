## Prompt — format **1:1**, 1600 × 1600

```
Image 1 is a crop from a larger hand-drawn isometric map of a small French town. Redraw image 1 as a sharp, high-resolution, richly detailed illustration.

KEEP EXACTLY: the same art style, outlines and colours, and the same composition and framing. Every building, roof, chimney, street, hedge and tree, every haystack, the windmill, the greenhouse stays at exactly the same place, with the same size, the same shape and the same viewing angle. Keep the colour of every roof, wall and awning exactly as in image 1. Everything that touches an edge of image 1 touches the same edge at the same place in your image. Do not zoom in or out, do not shift, crop, rotate or extend the view, do not add or remove any building.

ADD DETAILS that were too small to see: window frames, doors, roof tiles, ivy leaves, cobblestones, flower boxes, lamp posts, benches.

ADD PEOPLE in the same hand-drawn style: about 12 small townspeople, each about as tall as a ground-floor door: gardeners in overalls and caps in the vegetable plots, the miller at the door of the windmill. They wear ordinary clothes and must look clearly different from the story characters: exactly five figures have their arms stretched out: three scarecrows with a straw hat and a CHECKED shirt, one scarecrow without a hat and with a CHECKED shirt, and the convict with a straw hat and a STRIPED shirt; nobody else wears a straw hat, stripes or checks.

STORY (draw every element exactly as described, clearly visible, never hidden behind a tree, a building or another person):
- The scarecrow shown on image 2 (on a wooden pole, arms stretched out, straw hat, blond straw hair, white shirt with blue CHECKS, grey trousers): draw THREE identical scarecrows of this kind, far apart from each other: two in the hay field left of the windmill and one in the vegetable plots at the bottom.
- The hatless scarecrow shown on image 3 (the same scarecrow with NO hat, its blond straw hair blowing in the wind): draw ONE, standing in the vegetable plots at the bottom.
- The convict in disguise shown on image 4 (straw hat, spiky blond hair, white shirt with blue STRIPES, grey trousers) stands perfectly still in the hay field in front of the windmill, his arms stretched out, pretending to be a scarecrow, his feet on the ground (no pole), with a blue sweat drop on his forehead and three pigeons pecking at his feet.

No written words anywhere: every sign stays blank. No frame, no caption.
```
