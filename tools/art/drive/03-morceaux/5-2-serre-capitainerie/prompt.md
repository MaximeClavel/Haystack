## Prompt — format **1:1**, 1600 × 1600

```
Image 1 is a crop from a larger hand-drawn isometric map of a small French town. Redraw image 1 as a sharp, high-resolution, richly detailed illustration.

KEEP EXACTLY: the same art style, outlines and colours, and the same composition and framing. Every building, roof, chimney, street, hedge and tree, every haystack, the greenhouse, the bridge, crate, flag stays at exactly the same place, with the same size, the same shape and the same viewing angle. Keep the colour of every roof, wall and awning exactly as in image 1. Everything that touches an edge of image 1 touches the same edge at the same place in your image. Do not zoom in or out, do not shift, crop, rotate or extend the view, do not add or remove any building.

ADD DETAILS that were too small to see: window frames, doors, roof tiles, ivy leaves, cobblestones, flower boxes, lamp posts, benches.

ADD PEOPLE in the same hand-drawn style: about 20 small townspeople, each about as tall as a ground-floor door: gardeners in the vegetable plots, dockers near the warehouse, a sailor on the quay. They wear ordinary clothes and must look clearly different from the story characters: no scarecrow here.

STORY (draw every element exactly as described, clearly visible, never hidden behind a tree, a building or another person):
- A tiny shiny golden sewing needle (a little golden stroke with a sparkle) is planted upright in the soil just in front of the glass greenhouse.
- The stone house on the quay with the flags next to it is the harbour master's office: put the sign "CAPITAINERIE" above its door; a life buoy hangs next to the door, with a tiny shiny golden sewing needle (a little golden stroke with a sparkle) stuck in it.

The only written words in the image are: "CAPITAINERIE" (spelled exactly); every other sign stays blank. No frame, no caption.
```
