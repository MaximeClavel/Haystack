```
Image 1 is a crop from a larger hand-drawn isometric map of a small French town. Redraw image 1 as a sharp, high-resolution, richly detailed illustration.

KEEP EXACTLY: the same art style, outlines and colours, and the same composition and framing. Every building, roof, chimney, street, hedge and tree, every boat, the river, crate, the crane stays at exactly the same place, with the same size, the same shape and the same viewing angle. Keep the colour of every roof, wall and awning exactly as in image 1. Everything that touches an edge of image 1 touches the same edge at the same place in your image. Do not zoom in or out, do not shift, crop, rotate or extend the view, do not add or remove any building.

ADD DETAILS that were too small to see: window frames, doors, roof tiles, ivy leaves, cobblestones, flower boxes, lamp posts, benches.

ADD PEOPLE in the same hand-drawn style: about 30 small townspeople, each about as tall as a ground-floor door: spectators in evening dress, shocked, on the theatre steps and the forecourt, dockers on the quay. They wear ordinary clothes and must look clearly different from the story characters: nobody else wears a top hat, orange trousers or a red jacket; nobody else reads a newspaper.

STORY (draw every element exactly as described, clearly visible, never hidden behind a tree, a building or another person):
- On the paved forecourt at the foot of the theatre steps, the diva shown on image 2 (long black hair, long red dress) has collapsed on her back, eyes closed, a small glass bottle in one hand, a bouquet of flowers fallen beside her, with a small dizzy-stars bubble above her head.
- The policeman shown on image 3 (navy kepi, big ginger moustache, blue uniform jacket, beige Bermuda shorts, red-and-yellow striped scarf) crouches beside her, worried, with a blue sweat drop above his head.
- A few steps away, near the lamp post on the quay, the old actor shown on image 4 (black top hat, grey moustache, RED suit jacket, ORANGE trousers, closed black umbrella) stands calmly reading an open newspaper, not looking at her, his closed umbrella hooked on his arm.

No written words anywhere: every sign stays blank. No frame, no caption.
```
