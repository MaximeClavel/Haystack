## Prompt — format **1:1**, 1600 × 1600

```
Image 1 is a crop from a larger hand-drawn isometric map of a small French town. Redraw image 1 as a sharp, high-resolution, richly detailed illustration.

KEEP EXACTLY: the same art style, outlines and colours, and the same composition and framing. Every building, roof, chimney, street, hedge and tree, every market stall, the fountain stays at exactly the same place, with the same size, the same shape and the same viewing angle. Keep the colour of every roof, wall and awning exactly as in image 1. Everything that touches an edge of image 1 touches the same edge at the same place in your image. Do not zoom in or out, do not shift, crop, rotate or extend the view, do not add or remove any building.

ADD DETAILS that were too small to see: window frames, doors, roof tiles, ivy leaves, cobblestones, flower boxes, lamp posts, benches.

ADD PEOPLE in the same hand-drawn style: about 35 small townspeople, each about as tall as a ground-floor door: people strolling on the square, a wedding party on the town hall steps, customers at the café tables, a waiter. They wear ordinary clothes and must look clearly different from the story characters: nobody else wears a top hat, a purple suit, a yellow coat or a police uniform; nobody else carries a tart.

STORY (draw every element exactly as described, clearly visible, never hidden behind a tree, a building or another person):
- On the square in front of the town hall, next to the bench under its windows, the old woman shown on image 2 (grey bun, no hat, yellow checked coat, green skirt, handbag) waves with one hand (empty hands), and right beside her the policeman shown on image 3 (navy kepi, big ginger moustache, blue uniform jacket, beige Bermuda shorts, red-and-yellow striped scarf) happily holds a strawberry tart on a plate, with a small red heart bubble above his head.
- Three small round café tables with chairs and a parasol stand on the cobblestones along the hedge, in the lower middle of the square (a café terrace). Standing at this terrace, the SAME old woman is shown a second time (earlier in the story), carrying a strawberry tart on a plate with both hands.
- The jeweller shown on image 4 (black top hat, round glasses, purple suit, walking cane) tiptoes sneakily along the street at the bottom of the image, heading left, holding a strawberry tart on a plate up in front of his face to hide.

No written words anywhere: every sign stays blank. No frame, no caption.
```
