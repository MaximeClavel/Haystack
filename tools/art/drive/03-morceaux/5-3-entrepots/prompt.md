## Prompt — format **1:1**, 1600 × 1600

```
Image 1 is a crop from a larger hand-drawn isometric map of a small French town. Redraw image 1 as a sharp, high-resolution, richly detailed illustration.

KEEP EXACTLY: the same art style, outlines and colours, and the same composition and framing. Every building, roof, chimney, street, hedge and tree, every boat, the river, crate, the iron footbridge stays at exactly the same place, with the same size, the same shape and the same viewing angle. Keep the colour of every roof, wall and awning exactly as in image 1. Everything that touches an edge of image 1 touches the same edge at the same place in your image. Do not zoom in or out, do not shift, crop, rotate or extend the view, do not add or remove any building.

ADD DETAILS that were too small to see: window frames, doors, roof tiles, ivy leaves, cobblestones, flower boxes, lamp posts, benches.

ADD PEOPLE in the same hand-drawn style: about 25 small townspeople, each about as tall as a ground-floor door: dockers carrying sacks and rolling barrels near the warehouses, rowers in the boats. They wear ordinary clothes and must look clearly different from the story characters: nobody else wears a top hat, orange trousers or a red jacket.

STORY (draw every element exactly as described, clearly visible, never hidden behind a tree, a building or another person):
- The old actor shown on image 2 (black top hat, grey moustache, RED suit jacket, ORANGE trousers, closed black umbrella) is running along the stone quay at the top right, between the crates, towards the right, holding his closed umbrella and looking back over his shoulder.

No written words anywhere: every sign stays blank. No frame, no caption.
```
