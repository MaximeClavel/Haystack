## Prompt — format **1:1**, 1600 × 1600

```
Image 1 is a crop from a larger hand-drawn isometric map of a small French town. Redraw image 1 as a sharp, high-resolution, richly detailed illustration.

KEEP EXACTLY: the same art style, outlines and colours, and the same composition and framing. Every building, roof, chimney, street, hedge and tree, every haystack stays at exactly the same place, with the same size, the same shape and the same viewing angle. Keep the colour of every roof, wall and awning exactly as in image 1. Everything that touches an edge of image 1 touches the same edge at the same place in your image. Do not zoom in or out, do not shift, crop, rotate or extend the view, do not add or remove any building.

ADD DETAILS that were too small to see: window frames, doors, roof tiles, ivy leaves, cobblestones, flower boxes, lamp posts, benches.

ADD PEOPLE in the same hand-drawn style: about 40 small townspeople, each about as tall as a ground-floor door: travellers with suitcases, porters with trolleys, people sitting on the benches. They wear ordinary clothes and must look clearly different from the story characters: nobody else wears a beret, a pink dress, sunglasses or a police uniform.

STORY (draw every element exactly as described, clearly visible, never hidden behind a tree, a building or another person):
- The stone building with the clock tower is the railway station: put the sign "GARE DE MEULEBOURG" above its columned entrance.
- A navy blue police van marked "POLICE" is parked in the street right at the foot of the station steps, its rear doors wide open and empty inside.
- On the paving next to the van, the young woman shown on image 2 (red beret, sunglasses, curly ginger-red hair, pink dress with white polka dots) lies flat on her back, pretending to have fainted, one hand on her forehead, with a small dizzy-stars bubble above her head.
- Crouching beside her, the policeman shown on image 3 (navy kepi, big ginger moustache, blue uniform jacket, beige Bermuda shorts, red-and-yellow striped scarf), worried, with a blue sweat drop above his head, his back turned to the van.
- On the LEFT clock face of the tower there are THREE hands: the third one is a tiny shiny golden sewing needle.

The only written words in the image are: "GARE DE MEULEBOURG", "POLICE" (spelled exactly); every other sign stays blank. No frame, no caption.
```
