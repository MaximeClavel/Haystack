## Prompt — format **1:1**, 1600 × 1600

```
Image 1 is a crop from a larger hand-drawn isometric map of a small French town. Redraw image 1 as a sharp, high-resolution, richly detailed illustration.

KEEP EXACTLY: the same art style, outlines and colours, and the same composition and framing. Every building, roof, chimney, street, hedge and tree, every crate, barrel, the pond stays at exactly the same place, with the same size, the same shape and the same viewing angle. Keep the colour of every roof, wall and awning exactly as in image 1. Everything that touches an edge of image 1 touches the same edge at the same place in your image. Do not zoom in or out, do not shift, crop, rotate or extend the view, do not add or remove any building.

ADD DETAILS that were too small to see: window frames, doors, roof tiles, ivy leaves, cobblestones, flower boxes, lamp posts, benches.

ADD PEOPLE in the same hand-drawn style: about 25 small townspeople, each about as tall as a ground-floor door: people strolling, picnicking on the lawn, a painter at an easel, children with a dog. They wear ordinary clothes and must look clearly different from the story characters.

STORY (draw every element exactly as described, clearly visible, never hidden behind a tree, a building or another person):
- The bronze statue on the pedestal is the town founder: he raises one hand high and holds a tiny shiny golden sewing needle (a little golden stroke with a sparkle) in it.
- The white marquee of the cream art deco cinema reads "LE REX".

The only written words in the image are: "LE REX" (spelled exactly); every other sign stays blank. No frame, no caption.
```

- Rien n'a bougé par rapport au morceau (bâtiments, rues, bords), les couleurs des toits et des murs sont les mêmes.
- La statue lève une main qui tient une aiguille dorée.
