```

KEEP EXACTLY: the same art style, outlines and colours, and the same composition and framing. Every building, roof, chimney, street, hedge and tree, every haystack, tombstone, cypress stays at exactly the same place, with the same size, the same shape and the same viewing angle. Keep the colour of every roof, wall and awning exactly as in image 1. Everything that touches an edge of image 1 touches the same edge at the same place in your image. Do not zoom in or out, do not shift, crop, rotate or extend the view, do not add or remove any building.

ADD DETAILS that were too small to see: window frames, doors, roof tiles, ivy leaves, cobblestones, flower boxes, lamp posts, benches.

ADD PEOPLE in the same hand-drawn style: about 14 small townspeople, each about as tall as a ground-floor door: farm workers in flat caps making hay with pitchforks in the fields, a gardener with a wheelbarrow and an old lady with flowers in the cemetery. They wear ordinary clothes and must look clearly different from the story characters: nobody else wears a top hat or orange trousers.

STORY (draw every element exactly as described, clearly visible, never hidden behind a tree, a building or another person):
- In the walled cemetery, in its upper half, the actress shown on image 2 (black top hat, long ginger-red hair, long green coat, ORANGE trousers, walking cane) stands between two tombstones, leaning on her cane with one hand and holding an open script in the other, reciting theatrically, with a small music-notes bubble above her head.
- The tall grey monument standing among the tombstones is a stone angel statue; the angel holds a tiny shiny golden sewing needle (a little golden stroke with a sparkle) in its hand.

No written words anywhere: every sign stays blank. No frame, no caption.
```
