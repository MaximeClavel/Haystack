## Prompt — format **1:1**, 1600 × 1600

```
Image 1 is a crop from a larger hand-drawn isometric map of a small French town. Redraw image 1 as a sharp, high-resolution, richly detailed illustration.

KEEP EXACTLY: the same art style, outlines and colours, and the same composition and framing. Every building, roof, chimney, street, hedge and tree stays at exactly the same place, with the same size, the same shape and the same viewing angle. Keep the colour of every roof, wall and awning exactly as in image 1. Everything that touches an edge of image 1 touches the same edge at the same place in your image. Do not zoom in or out, do not shift, crop, rotate or extend the view, do not add or remove any building.

ADD DETAILS that were too small to see: window frames, doors, roof tiles, ivy leaves, cobblestones, flower boxes, lamp posts, benches.

ADD PEOPLE in the same hand-drawn style: about 35 small townspeople, each about as tall as a ground-floor door: hotel guests and porters with luggage under the hotel awnings, pharmacists at their doors, shoppers, a postman. They wear ordinary clothes and must look clearly different from the story characters: no police uniform with Bermuda shorts here.

STORY (draw every element exactly as described, clearly visible, never hidden behind a tree, a building or another person):
- The large building with the awnings at the top is a hotel: put the sign "GRAND HÔTEL" above its awnings.
- The red brick building at the left edge is the police station: the sign "COMMISSARIAT" above its columned door.

The only written words in the image are: "GRAND HÔTEL", "COMMISSARIAT" (spelled exactly); every other sign stays blank. No frame, no caption.
```

## À vérifier

- Rien n'a bougé par rapport au morceau (bâtiments, rues, bords), les couleurs des toits et des murs sont les mêmes.
- Enseignes « GRAND HÔTEL » et « COMMISSARIAT ».
