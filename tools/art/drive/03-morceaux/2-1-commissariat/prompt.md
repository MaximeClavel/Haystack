## Prompt — format **1:1**, 1600 × 1600

```
Image 1 is a crop from a larger hand-drawn isometric map of a small French town. Redraw image 1 as a sharp, high-resolution, richly detailed illustration.

KEEP EXACTLY: the same art style, outlines and colours, and the same composition and framing. Every building, roof, chimney, street, hedge and tree, every tombstone stays at exactly the same place, with the same size, the same shape and the same viewing angle. Keep the colour of every roof, wall and awning exactly as in image 1. Everything that touches an edge of image 1 touches the same edge at the same place in your image. Do not zoom in or out, do not shift, crop, rotate or extend the view, do not add or remove any building.

ADD DETAILS that were too small to see: window frames, doors, roof tiles, ivy leaves, cobblestones, flower boxes, lamp posts, benches.

ADD PEOPLE in the same hand-drawn style: about 30 small townspeople, each about as tall as a ground-floor door: police officers in navy uniforms and kepis around the police station, shoppers at the little shops with awnings, a gardener in the cemetery. They wear ordinary clothes and must look clearly different from the story characters: no policeman with a ginger moustache and Bermuda shorts here.

STORY (draw every element exactly as described, clearly visible, never hidden behind a tree, a building or another person):
- The red brick building is the police station: put the sign "COMMISSARIAT" above its columned door and a blue lamp next to it.
- Three white-and-blue police cars are parked side by side in the street in front of the police station; a tiny shiny golden sewing needle (a little golden stroke with a sparkle) lies on the ground between two of them.

The only written words in the image are: "COMMISSARIAT" (spelled exactly); every other sign stays blank. No frame, no caption.
```

## À vérifier

- Enseigne « COMMISSARIAT » sur le bâtiment en brique.
