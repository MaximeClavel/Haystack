## Prompt — format **1:1**

```
Same art style, proportions, outlines and soft painted shading as the attached character sheet. Image 2 shows a character. Draw the same scarecrow (cloth face, blue-CHECKED white shirt, grey trousers, arms stretched out on its pole) with NO hat, its spiky blond straw hair blowing in the wind. Everything not mentioned stays identical to image 2. Draw this ONE character twice on the same image, side by side, same scale: once in three-quarter front view and once in three-quarter back view, standing, full body, natural colours. Plain white background, generous empty space, no ground shadow, no text.
```

Enregistre l'image réussie dans ce dossier sous le nom `resultat.png` : elle sera jointe aux sections de la carte où il apparaît.
